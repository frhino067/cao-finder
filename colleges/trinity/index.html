How to compare Trinity courses

Start with the compulsory first-year modules rather than the course title alone. Compare how much time each programme gives to essays, practical work, mathematics, languages, placements or group projects.

Points are not fixed

The figures below are a 2026 snapshot. CAO points reflect demand and available places in a particular round, so they can rise or fall. Rank choices in genuine order of preference and separately check every subject requirement.
