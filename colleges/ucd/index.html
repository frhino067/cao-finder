Watch for broad entry routes

Several UCD courses begin with a broad entry route before students specialise. Check the programme structure, when choices are made and whether places in later subjects are guaranteed or competitive.

Compare the complete college week

Modules matter most, but also investigate timetable patterns, commuting, campus location, placements and course-specific costs. These practical details can separate two courses that look similar in a prospectus.
