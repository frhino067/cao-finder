CAO entry requirements: why enough points may not be enough

A points figure answers only one part of the admissions question. Before saving a course as a serious option, check that your subjects and qualifications meet its requirements.

THE TAKEAWAY

Treat eligibility as a checklist of exact conditions, then consider points. A points filter cannot confirm that a college will accept your application.

Separate the three checks

For each course, look for general college entry requirements, course-specific requirements and any extra assessment or application conditions. The CAO handbook tells applicants to verify minimum entry requirements with institutions and explains that restricted courses may involve additional assessments.

Read these as separate boxes. Meeting one does not automatically meet the others. A course page may link to a general admissions page, so follow that link instead of stopping at the short summary beside the course title.

Copy exact wording into an eligibility sheet

CheckWhat to record
IdentityCourse code, campus, level and entry year
General requirementsNumber of subjects, levels and minimum grades
Named subjectsRequired subject and exact grade
Extra stepsAssessment, portfolio or other listed condition
UncertaintyYour question, source link and date checked

Use “confirmed”, “not met” and “need to ask” rather than a single yes/no field. An unresolved condition should stay visible until you have an answer.

Read “and” and “or” carefully

Consider a fictional requirement: “H4 in Mathematics and H5 in one laboratory science.” That asks for both conditions. “H4 in Mathematics or H4 in Applied Mathematics” describes alternatives. These examples are illustrations only and are not the requirements of any named course.

Make sure a note about an exemption, alternative qualification or special route actually applies to you. A friend’s circumstances or an older prospectus might be different. Save the current official page and ask for clarification when two pages appear inconsistent.

Send a question the admissions office can answer

Use a short message: “I am considering [course code] for [entry year]. I take [relevant subjects and levels]. The entry page says [brief requirement]. Does [specific issue] meet this condition?” Avoid sending more personal information than the question needs.

If the reply points you to a document, read the relevant section and update your sheet. Keep the answer alongside the course code so you do not accidentally apply it to another programme at the same college.

Use the finder as the start of the check

CAO Finder can narrow courses by points, college and category. Its subject and duration filters cover a limited verified subset, and unknown details remain unknown. A course missing from a filtered result is not proof that you are ineligible.

Open the college page for every serious choice, check the current year, and repeat the eligibility review before finalising your application. Use the CAO applicant resources to check any restrictions or supporting-document instructions relevant to your situation.

ONE MORE THING

Can bonus maths points make up for a required maths grade?

The bonus changes a points score, not your grade. Check the exact required grade and any accepted alternatives with the college.
