Business courses are not all the same

Read the module list for accounting, economics, marketing, analytics, law, languages and entrepreneurship. Two degrees with ‘business’ in the title can produce very different weekly work and specialist options.

Check placement and accreditation

If a course advertises a placement, confirm whether it is compulsory, how long it lasts and who finds it. For careers where professional exemptions matter, verify the exact accreditation with the college or professional body.
